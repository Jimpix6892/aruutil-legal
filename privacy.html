AruUtil Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AruUtil ("the Bot", "AruUtil", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use or interact with the Bot on Discord.

AruUtil is a personal Discord utility bot operated independently by its developer.

1. Information We May Process

AruUtil may process limited information made available to it through Discord's API when necessary for the Bot's features.

This may include:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames, display names, and avatars
  • Discord server IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord role IDs
  • Discord message content, when required for a specific Bot feature
  • Discord interaction information, such as slash commands and buttons
  • Information contained in commands or other messages sent to the Bot

AruUtil only accesses information necessary for its functionality and only to the extent permitted by Discord's API and the permissions granted to the Bot.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by AruUtil may be used to:

  • Respond to commands and interactions
  • Provide utility and moderation features
  • React to messages when a configured feature requires it
  • Manage server-specific Bot settings
  • Perform actions requested by users or server administrators
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Bot
  • Diagnose errors and technical problems
  • Prevent abuse or misuse of the Bot

AruUtil does not use information for advertising or sell personal information to third parties.

3. Message Content

Some AruUtil features may require access to message content.

When message content is processed, it is used only for the relevant Bot functionality. AruUtil does not intentionally create a permanent database of all messages sent in Discord.

4. Data Storage and Retention

AruUtil is designed to minimize data collection and storage. Information that does not need to be permanently stored is processed temporarily and discarded when it is no longer needed.

If server configuration or other information is stored to provide a feature, it may remain stored until the configuration is removed, the Bot is removed from the Discord server, the information is no longer necessary, or the developer removes it.

5. Third-Party Services

AruUtil operates through Discord's platform and APIs. Discord may independently collect and process information according to its own policies and terms.

AruUtil may also use third-party services necessary to host, operate, monitor, or maintain the Bot. Such services may process limited technical information required to provide their services.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by AruUtil. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

AruUtil is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children and does not knowingly request sensitive personal information from users.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning information associated with you.

You may contact the Bot's developer to request information about data associated with your Discord account or to request deletion where applicable.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as AruUtil's features or data practices change.

10. Contact

For questions, concerns, or privacy-related requests regarding AruUtil, contact the Bot's developer through the Discord account or server associated with AruUtil.
